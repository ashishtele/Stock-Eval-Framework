## Central conclusion

The transferable process is: understand the business, verify management execution and accounting, test that growth creates cash and returns on capital, pay a price that allows for disappointment, and size the position so one mistake does not dominate the portfolio.

The non-transferable narrative is: repeat a few historical multibaggers, concentrate almost everything in one theme, rely on tips or confidence, and expect a guaranteed result. Survivorship bias, hindsight, luck, unreported losers and missing cash-flow records prevent this interview from establishing a predictive investment model.
