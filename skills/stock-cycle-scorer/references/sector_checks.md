# Sector checks

Use these to decide what the two or three cycle variables are, and what public data
verifies management. Do not treat a sector label as evidence.

## Chemicals

- Spreads: product price minus key raw material. Track the spread, not the headline
  realisation.
- Chinese dumping: import volumes and prices; anti-dumping actions.
- Inventory gains: a margin jump in the first good quarter can be stock revaluation, not
  demand. Check whether the margin held in the next quarter.
- Specialty vs commodity: labels are marketing until pricing power and customer
  qualification are shown.

## Pipes / building materials

- Easy-entry overcapacity: look at industry capacity additions and utilisation.
- Resin prices: PVC/resin is the swing input. Falling resin can lift margins temporarily.
- Government spending: housing, irrigation, plumbing and infrastructure budgets.
- Unorganised players exiting when input costs spike is a **supply shock**, not proof of a
  demand cycle. Volume growth is the demand evidence.
- Watch working capital and channel inventory; a price-led recovery with flat volume is
  weaker than a volume-led one.

## Pharma / CDMO

- Heavy capex phase: capex/sales step-up, CWIP, depreciation drag before revenue.
- US FDA Drug Master File (DMF) filings are public proof of capability.
- FDA inspections: observations, Form 483s, import alerts.
- Innovator pipelines, peptides/ADCs, and long-term supply agreements.
- CDMO share of revenue and its margin versus the base business.

## Aerospace / defence

- Supplier tiers: Tier 1 vs Tier 2/3, and whether the company is sole/primary supplier.
- AS9100 and Nadcap certifications.
- OEM build rates and order books.
- "Aerospace" without certifications scores capability <= 1.

## IT services

- Outcome-based vs hours-based billing.
- Domain specialisation and wallet share.
- Guidance versus sector growth.
- Large caps re-rate late; a small-cap pivot can re-rate earlier.
- Market skepticism can sit in the price even when analysts are friendly.

## Auto ancillary

- Non-auto mix and its margins.
- EV transition exposure and content per vehicle.
- OEM concentration and platform wins.

## Any sector

Name the 2-3 cycle variables and the public data that verifies management. If you cannot
name them, the sector section is not done.
