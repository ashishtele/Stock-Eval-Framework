# Time horizon and valuation

## Time horizon

The video is explicit about a **three-year** view `[00:53:40]`. The point is not
that three years is sacred; it is that you must articulate:

- your time horizon
- your investing framework
- what would make you sell
- what return you are underwriting

A stock can be a good business and a bad three-year investment if the catalyst is
too far away or the entry price already discounts it.

## Valuation

“Don't lead with valuation.” `[00:56:58]`

The argument is:

- a high P/E can be cheap if the earnings band is about to change
- a low P/E can be expensive if there is no growth
- at the first inflection, the market has not yet converged on the new earnings
  trajectory
- most investors over-analyse the last 5–10 years and under-analyse the future

This does not mean valuation is irrelevant. It means P/E should not be the first
filter. Record it as context, not as a gate.
