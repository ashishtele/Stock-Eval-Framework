# Funnel and watchlist

The video describes a repeatable process `[00:45:19]`:

1. identify a theme
2. scan for candidates
3. narrow the funnel
4. check the strengths
5. stop screening and start reading
6. build a watchlist
7. apply the framework
8. invest with conviction

The screen is only steps 2–4. It cannot do the reading for you.

## What to read after the screen

- the last two or three concalls
- the investor presentation
- the latest annual report
- exchange announcements for orders, capex and management changes
- peer results and industry capacity data
- the company's own guidance versus delivery

## Watchlist fields

For each name, record:

- theme and catalyst
- stage: Train A / B / C
- J-curve evidence and date
- what would make the thesis stronger
- what would falsify it
- next result date
- entry zone or base
- position sizing note

A watchlist is not a buy list. It is a queue for reading and tracking.

## Strength in adversity

The video gives the tariff example `[00:45:58]`: after tariffs, look at which
exporters' margins did **not** collapse. That is evidence of business-model
strength. It is a separate qualitative check, not a financial ratio in Screener.
