# Market and index stage

The Train A/B/C idea is not only for individual stocks. The video applies it to
markets and market segments `[01:17:17]`:

- the Nifty can be in Train A: no visible catalyst
- many mid-caps can be in Train B: a catalyst is visible but not yet in the numbers
- many small-caps can be in Train C: the move is already under way

This matters because a stock-level screen can look strong while its market or
segment is extended. Record the market stage separately from the stock stage.

## Context fields

```
market_stage: TRAIN_A | TRAIN_B | TRAIN_C | UNKNOWN
market_stage_note
```

Do not use the market stage to override the stock's J-curve or quality gates.
It is a top-down risk note.

## Theme win-rate

The video cites a healthcare sector win-rate of roughly 63–65% over two years
`[01:09:34]`–`[01:10:16]`. The point is to study sectors where the base rate is
favourable, not to assume every stock in the sector will work. If the sector win
rate is high, spend more time there; still underwrite the individual business.
