# Catalyst taxonomy

The video spells out a catalyst checklist using the word CATALYST. Use these tags
only when there is an observable event with a source and date. A catalyst is not
a theme, a hope, or a management adjective.

| Tag | Meaning | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `acquisition_alliance` | A good acquisition, JV, or alliance | Exchange filing, deal terms, acquired revenue/capability |
| `turnaround` | Earnings had not turned for several quarters, then did | Quarterly results, concall explanation |
| `approval_policy` | Approval, licence, PLI, ALMM, regulation, policy change | Government notification, approval letter |
| `large_order` | A large order versus the company's revenue base | Exchange announcement with order value and duration |
| `year_zero` | Entry into a new high-growth vertical | New segment disclosure, first revenue, capex announcement |
| `switch` | Shift to a higher-margin business or a new management | Segment mix, margin bridge, board/management change |
| `theme_tailwind` | A sector theme with a measurable tailwind | Industry data, policy, order pipeline, peer results |
| `new_capex` | New capacity or a new plant | Capex announcement, CWIP, commissioning update |
| `new_product` | A new product or customer that changes the earnings band | Launch, customer win, segment disclosure |
| `new_management` | A new promoter/CEO with a credible track record | Board filing, open offer, management interview |

## Rules

- A tag needs a source. If the only evidence is a price move or a social-media
  post, do not tag it.
- A large order must be large relative to the company's revenue, not just large
  in absolute terms.
- A theme is not a catalyst for a specific company until the company has a
  measurable link to it.
- `new_capex` is a Train B signal until the capacity is commissioned and shows up
  in revenue/margins.
- Do not upgrade `CANDIDATE` to `TRAIN_C` on a catalyst alone. The J-curve and
  quality gates still need to pass.
